Font files included in this package are subsets of Noto Sans and Noto Sans CJK JP.

The Noto fonts are licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1.
Project information:
https://github.com/notofonts
https://scripts.sil.org/OFL

The subset files are included only to provide the characters required by the
VEKMaker Name Plate Generator:
- Latin characters used in English and Portuguese
- Hiragana and katakana
- Basic punctuation and numbers

Kanji is intentionally not included.
