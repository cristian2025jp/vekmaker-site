Perguntas frequentes

Qual material é mais indicado?

PETG is generally more suitable for clips that must flex repeatedly. PLA can work for gentle snap fits, but narrow openings and thick walls may cause it to crack.

Qual largura de abertura devo usar?

Start with an opening around 70% to 85% of the fitted cable diameter. Increase it for rigid materials or cables that should be removed frequently.

Como o clipe deve ser impresso?

The STL is exported with the flat base on the print bed and the cable openings facing upward. This normally avoids support material.
