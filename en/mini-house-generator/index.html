Frequently asked questions

How many STL files are generated?

Five: base, walls, roof, door and window.

Are the walls separated?

No. The front, back and side walls are exported together as one wall piece to simplify printing and assembly.

How are the door and window installed?

They are designed to be inserted from inside the house toward the outside. A larger rear flange stops each part at the correct depth and makes gluing easier.

Does the base help with alignment?

Yes. Small guide posts on the base sit inside the wall corners and help position the one-piece wall section.

Can each part be printed in a different color?

Yes. Each main component is downloaded separately so it can be printed in a different color without repeated filament changes.
