FAQ

How does this box work?

It uses two printable parts: an outer sleeve and an inner tray that slides in and out like a matchbox.

Do I need to adjust wall thickness or sliding clearance?

No. This simplified generator uses fixed construction values so you only need to choose the useful inside size and a few visual options.

What is the finger notch for?

The optional notch makes it easier to pull the inner tray out of the sleeve.

What does the divider option do?

The 2-compartment option adds one centered divider to the inner tray.

How should the parts be printed?

The tray is designed to print upright on its bottom. The sleeve STL is exported standing on one open end to reduce support requirements.
