Frequently asked questions

Are the dimensions internal?

Yes. Width, depth and height refer to the usable internal dimensions. The external size is calculated automatically from the wall and bottom thickness.

How are the handles created?

Two horizontal handle openings are automatically placed near the top of the shorter sides and scale with the selected depth.

What does the decorative style change?

Smooth has no detail. Recessed Panel creates a shallow flat inset area on the faces.

What is the corner radius?

The corner radius is fixed at 2 mm to keep the geometry simple and consistent.

Is this a one-piece print?

Yes. The base and all four walls are generated as one printable storage box STL.
