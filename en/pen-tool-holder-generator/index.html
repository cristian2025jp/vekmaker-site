FAQ

What can this holder be used for?

It can hold pens, markers, brushes, craft tools, small screwdrivers and other cylindrical tools that fit the selected hole diameter.

How is the hole diameter calculated?

The generator adds the selected clearance to the measured pen or tool diameter. For example, a 12 mm tool with 1 mm clearance creates a 13 mm hole.

Why are the holes separate instead of one large compartment?

Individual holes keep pens and tools upright and separated, making them easier to organize and grab.

How should the holder be printed?

Print it upright with the flat bottom on the build plate. The holes are vertical, so the model normally prints without support.
