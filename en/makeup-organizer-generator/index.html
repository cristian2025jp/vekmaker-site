FAQ

Why was the drawer removed?

This simplified version is intended to reduce print time and make the model easier to print for beginners.

What does the front and back height option change?

It creates a lower front and a higher back, making the organizer more practical for makeup, brushes and small desk items.

Can I still change the number of compartments?

Yes. The default is 3 rows by 3 columns, but you can change rows and columns to suit your needs.

Does this version use less support?

Yes. The organizer is now a single open-top body with a flat bottom, designed to be simpler and more support-friendly than the drawer version.

What is the best way to test it?

First open the STL in Bambu Studio, check for support needs and slicing behavior, and if needed print a smaller test before using a full-size version.
