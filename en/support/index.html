Support VEKMaker

Help keep VEKMaker free

VEKMaker is free to use. If the tools have helped you, you can support continued development with a voluntary contribution.

Why support VEKMaker?

VEKMaker is developed as a free collection of browser-based tools for creating customizable STL files. Support helps with the time and resources needed to maintain the site, improve existing generators and create new ones.

A small contribution helps

There is no payment required to use the generators. Supporting the project is completely optional and is simply a way to help VEKMaker continue growing.
