Privacy Policy

How information is handled when you visit VEKMaker and use its browser-based generators.

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how VEKMaker handles information when you visit the website and use its STL generators.

Information you provide

VEKMaker does not currently require an account. Values entered into the generators, such as dimensions and model options, are processed directly in your browser and are not intentionally sent to VEKMaker for storage.

Automatically collected information

VEKMaker uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how the website is used, such as which pages are visited, how visitors reach the site, general device and browser information, and approximate geographic information. This data helps improve the website and its generators. Google Analytics may process information such as IP address and online identifiers in accordance with Google's own privacy practices.

Cookies and local storage

VEKMaker does not use local storage to save generator settings. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies to measure website usage and distinguish visits. Analytics is not loaded during VEKMaker's local development environment. Browser settings and applicable privacy controls may be used to manage cookies.

Google Analytics

VEKMaker uses Google Analytics 4, a service provided by Google, to collect aggregated usage statistics and understand traffic sources and website performance. VEKMaker does not intentionally send the dimensions or model options entered into STL generators to Google Analytics.

External links

VEKMaker may contain links to external websites. Their privacy practices are controlled by their respective operators, and VEKMaker is not responsible for their content or policies.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website, its services or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact
