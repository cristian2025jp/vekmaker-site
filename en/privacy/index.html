Privacy Policy

How information is handled when you visit VEKMaker and use its browser-based generators.

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how VEKMaker handles information when you visit the website and use its STL generators.

Information you provide

VEKMaker does not currently require an account. Values entered into the generators, such as dimensions and model options, are processed directly in your browser and are not intentionally sent to VEKMaker for storage.

Automatically collected information

The website is hosted through third-party infrastructure. The hosting provider may automatically process technical data, such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages and access times, for security, reliability and operational purposes.

Cookies and local storage

VEKMaker does not currently use cookies or local storage to save generator settings. If analytics, advertising or other features that use cookies are added in the future, this policy will be updated as appropriate.

External links

VEKMaker may contain links to external websites. Their privacy practices are controlled by their respective operators, and VEKMaker is not responsible for their content or policies.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website, its services or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to scvbrasil@gmail.com.
