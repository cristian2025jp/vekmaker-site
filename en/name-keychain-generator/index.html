Frequently asked questions

Which dimensions are fixed?

The plate height is fixed at 30 mm, base thickness at 3 mm, text and symbol relief at 1 mm, and corner radius at 5 mm.

How is the plate length determined?

The generator measures the text and automatically adjusts the plate length. Very long names are reduced slightly to stay within a practical size.

How can I print two colors?

Download the base and the text + symbol as separate STL files. They are designed as separate bodies so you can assign different colors or print and glue them separately.

Which characters can I use?

You can choose Latin / Portuguese, Hiragana or Katakana. The three rounded font styles are available for Latin text; Japanese mode automatically uses a compatible Japanese font. Kanji is not supported yet.

What symbols are available?

Heart, star, flower, moon, lightning, paw and clover are available in this first version.
