Frequently asked questions

Is the bottle hollow?

No. The bottle is a solid decorative model with no internal access.

How does the flat label area work?

It creates a flat rounded-rectangle area on the front so you can later glue a name, emblem or another decorative piece.

Is the cap a separate piece?

Yes. The cap is exported separately and is intended to be glued to the neck after printing.

What is the cork effect?

The cork option adds a subtle irregular surface texture to the cap geometry while keeping it printable in one color.
