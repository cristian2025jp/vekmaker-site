FAQ

Which characters are supported?

The Latin option supports English, Portuguese accents and cedilla. The Japanese option supports hiragana and katakana. Kanji is not currently supported.

Why is kanji not supported?

Kanji requires a much larger font and often contains fine details that may be difficult to print reliably with a standard 0.4 mm nozzle.

What happens when the text is too large?

The generator automatically scales the text down to fit inside the plate while preserving a safe margin around the edges and mounting holes.
