FAQ

How is the petal effect created?

The outside wall uses a smooth repeating floral profile while the inside remains circular and practical.

Is wall thickness adjustable?

No. Wall and bottom thickness are fixed automatically to keep the model simple and printable.

How does the lid fit?

The lidded jar uses a simple matching petal-shaped cap with built-in printing clearance.

What can I use it for?

It works well for makeup brushes, cotton swabs, cotton pads, pens, jewelry and small desk items.

What does Petal Count change?

It changes the number of vertical floral lobes around the outside of the container.
