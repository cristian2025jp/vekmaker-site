FAQ

What is a favor box?

A small reusable container for candy, chocolates, keepsakes or small gifts at birthdays, weddings and other events.

How does the lid stay centered?

A locating lip under the lid fits inside the box opening. The clearance setting controls the fit.

What is Small Overhang?

It adds a little material around the lid edge so it is easier to grip and remove.

Can I add names or characters?

This generator creates only the reusable box structure. Names and themed decorations can be created separately.

How should I print it?

Print the box upright. The lid is exported with the broad top face on the build plate and the locating lip upward, so support is normally unnecessary.
