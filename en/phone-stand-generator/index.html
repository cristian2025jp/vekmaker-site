FAQ

What is Phone Slot Width?

It is the space reserved for the phone. Measure the total thickness of the phone and case, then add a small clearance if needed.

What does Back Angle control?

A lower value creates a more reclined phone position, while a higher value makes the phone more upright.

How should the stand be printed?

The STL is exported with the base on the print bed. Some dimensions may require support beneath the inclined back.
